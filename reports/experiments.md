# Experiments

_Generated by `elec experiments`. Selection folds 2025-03 to 2026-02 (12 monthly walk-forward folds, 17,509 half-hours, delivery days up to 2026-02-28). The hold-out (from 2026-03-01) is not read._

Each experiment is one change to the current model, motivated by a pattern in [data_patterns.md](data_patterns.md). The rule for adopting a change was fixed in DECISIONS.md before the first run:

1. the 95% moving-block bootstrap interval (7-day blocks, 2,000 resamples) of the mean daily reduction in pinball loss lies above zero;
2. pinball loss is lower in at least 9 of the 12 folds;
3. pooled P10-P90 coverage stays between 77% and 83%.

Pinball loss is in £/MWh, averaged over P10, P50 and P90; lower is better. The interval is for the mean daily reduction against the current model (positive means the change helps).

## What each experiment changed and what it showed

**`merit_order`** (pattern 2. The level drifts, and the merit order moves with it): add the slope of price on residual demand over the last 14 settled days, and the price that curve implies for each half-hour (relative to the 7-day mean). Pinball 4.370 against 4.330 (-0.9%; daily gain £-0.128 to £0.045), better in 5 of 12 folds, coverage 79.7%. It fails the rule: the interval includes zero or lies below it; better in only 5 of 12 folds.

**`recency_weight`** (pattern 2. The level drifts, and the merit order moves with it): weight training rows by recency (half-life 180 days). Pinball 4.383 against 4.330 (-1.2%; daily gain £-0.108 to £-0.001), better in 4 of 12 folds, coverage 79.6%. It fails the rule: the interval includes zero or lies below it; better in only 4 of 12 folds.

**`rolling_365d`** (pattern 2. The level drifts, and the merit order moves with it): train on the last 365 days instead of the expanding window. Pinball 4.433 against 4.330 (-2.4%; daily gain £-0.239 to £0.013), better in 4 of 12 folds, coverage 80.5%. It fails the rule: the interval includes zero or lies below it; better in only 4 of 12 folds.

**`monotone_residual_demand`** (pattern 3. Residual demand is the strongest single signal): constrain every tree to be non-decreasing in residual demand. It could not run as registered: `LightGBMError: Cannot use ``monotone_constraints`` in quantile objective, please disable it.`. It is reported as not run rather than replaced by a different change after the fact.

**`same_period_profile`** (pattern 4. The daily shape repeats): add the same half-hour's mean price over the last 7 settled days (level and relative to the 7-day mean). It fails the rule: the interval includes zero or lies below it; better in only 6 of 12 folds.

**`renewable_share`** (pattern 6. Renewables set the bottom of the distribution): add the forecast wind and solar share of demand. ## Adopted

Nothing passes the rule, so the current model stays as it is. The table is the evidence for its design: each alternative the data suggested was tried and did not beat it reliably.
